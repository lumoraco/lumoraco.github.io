LAMKO / LUMORA staff only

Enter the passphrase to open the interpreter. This device will remember it.

How to use this

  1. Tap English and speak normally. It keeps listening until you tap the same button again.
  2. The Korean appears in large type while you speak, and is read out loud when you finish a sentence.
  3. Tap 한국어 and hand the phone over when they answer.
  4. Change their language from the box at the top right — 14 languages.
Before you hand the phone to someone new: open ⋯ and tap New conversation. Otherwise they can scroll back and read what you discussed with the previous visitor.

Settings

Keys stay on this phone and are never part of the web page. The default service needs no account and answers in roughly a third of a second; a Google Cloud key is the same engine with a service guarantee behind it.

Conversation

Tap New conversation before handing the phone to the next visitor — otherwise they can scroll back and read the previous discussion. Copy or save it first if you want to keep it. The transcript never leaves this phone.
